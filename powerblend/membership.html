Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch between membership plans?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your membership at any time. Changes will take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
What if I need to cancel my membership?
You can cancel anytime with a 14-day notice. No cancellation fees or long-term contracts required. We'll miss you, but the option is there if you need it.
Do you offer corporate memberships?
Absolutely! We offer special corporate rates for groups. Contact our team at corporate@powerblend.com for more information.
Can I freeze my membership?
Yes, we offer up to 3 months of membership freeze per year. No charge during your freeze period.
Is there a first-time visitor discount?
Yes! First-time visitors get a free assessment and 50% off their first month when they sign up for a membership.
